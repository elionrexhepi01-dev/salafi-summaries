      </h2>

      <div class="space-y-6">

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          Allah the Almighty said:
        </p>

        <div class="bg-[#24211e] border-l-4 border-[#d6b278] p-6 sm:p-8 rounded-r-lg space-y-4">
          <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “And I (Allah) created not the Jinns and men except they should worship Me (Alone).”
          </p>
          <p class="text-sm sm:text-base text-[#d6b278]">
            (51:56)
          </p>
        </div>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          And He stated:
        </p>

        <div class="bg-[#24211e] border-l-4 border-[#d6b278] p-6 sm:p-8 rounded-r-lg space-y-4">
          <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “And verily, We have sent among every Ummah (community, nation) a Messenger (proclaiming): ‘Worship Allah (Alone), and avoid (or keep away from) Taghut (all false deities etc. i.e. do not worship Taghut besides Allah).’ ”
          </p>
          <p class="text-sm sm:text-base text-[#d6b278]">
            (16:36)
          </p>
        </div>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          And He said:
        </p>

        <div class="bg-[#24211e] border-l-4 border-[#d6b278] p-6 sm:p-8 rounded-r-lg space-y-4">
          <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “Worship Allah and join none with Him in worship.”
          </p>
          <p class="text-sm sm:text-base text-[#d6b278]">
            (4:36)
          </p>
        </div>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          And He said:
        </p>

        <div class="bg-[#24211e] border-l-4 border-[#d6b278] p-6 sm:p-8 rounded-r-lg space-y-4">
          <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “And your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him”
          </p>
          <p class="text-sm sm:text-base text-[#d6b278]">
            (17:23)
          </p>
        </div>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          Allah the Most Exalted said:
        </p>

        <div class="bg-[#24211e] border-l-4 border-[#d6b278] p-6 sm:p-8 rounded-r-lg space-y-4">
          <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “It is those who believe (in the Oneness of Allah and worship none but Him Alone) and confuse not their belief with Zulm (wrong i.e. by worshipping other besides Allah), for them (only) there is security and they are guided ones.”
          </p>
          <p class="text-sm sm:text-base text-[#d6b278]">
            (6:82)
          </p>
        </div>

      </div>

      <div class="space-y-6 border-t border-[#24211e] pt-8">

        </p>

        <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#332f2b] p-6 sm:p-8 rounded-lg space-y-4">
          <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “Whoever testifies that there is nothing worthy of worship in truth (no God) except Allah Alone, Who is without (peer or) partner, and that Muhammad is His slave and Messenger, and that ‘Iesa (Jesus) is the slave of Allah, His Messenger, and His Word which He bestowed in Maryam (Mary) and a spirit (created) from Him, and that Paradise & Hell-fire are realities, Allah will admit him into Paradise, whatever his deeds might be.”
          </p>
          <p class="text-sm sm:text-base text-[#d6b278]">
            (Sahih Al-Bukhari, Hadith No. 3252)
          </p>
        </div>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          The Prophet said:
        </p>

        <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#332f2b] p-6 sm:p-8 rounded-lg space-y-4">
          <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “Indeed Allah has forbidden for Hell the person who testifies: T here is nothing worthy of worship in truth (no true God) but Allah’, seeking thereby nothing but Allah’s Face.”
          </p>
          <p class="text-sm sm:text-base text-[#d6b278]">
            (Al-Bukhari, Muslim).
          </p>
        </div>

        <p class="text-lg leading-relaxed sm:text-xl">

        </p>

          <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">

          </p>
        </div>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">

        </p>

          <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">

          <p class="text-sm sm:text-base text-[#d6b278]">
            (4:48, 116)
          </p>
        </div>

        <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#332f2b] p-5 sm:p-6 rounded-lg space-y-2">
          <p class="text-lg leading-relaxed sm:text-xl">
            Prophet Ibrahim said: “And keep me and my sons away from worshipping idols.”
          </p>
          <p class="text-sm sm:text-base text-[#d6b278]">
            (14:35)
          </p>
        </div>

        <p class="text-lg leading-relaxed sm:text-xl">
          Ibn Mas‘ud narrated that Prophet Muhammad said:
        </p>

        <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] p-5 sm:p-6 rounded-r-lg">
          <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “Whoever dies while ascribing partners to Allah, enters the Hell-fire.”
          </p>
          <p class="mt-2 text-sm sm:text-base text-[#d6b278]">
            (Al-Bukhari)
          </p>
        </div>

        <p class="text-lg leading-relaxed sm:text-xl">
          Muslim reports from Jabir that Allah’s Messenger said:
        </p>

        <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] p-5 sm:p-6 rounded-r-lg">
          <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “Whoever meets Allah (on the Day of Judgement) not having associated anyone with Him (in worship), shall enter Paradise; and whoever meets Him having committed Shirk in any way will enter the Hell-fire.”
          </p>
        </div>

      </div>

              “A man entered Paradise because of a fly, and a man entered Hell-fire because of a fly.” They (the Companions) asked, “How was that possible O Messenger of Allah?” He said, “Two men passed by the people who had an idol by which they would not allow anyone to pass without making sacrifice to it. They ordered one man to make a sacrifice. He said, T have nothing to present as an offering.’ The people told him, ‘Sacrifice something, even if it be a fly.’ So he presented a fly (to their idol). They opened the way for him, and thus he entered the Hell-fire. They said to the other man, ‘Sacrifice something.’ He said, ‘I will never sacrifice anything to any other than Allah, Most Majestic and Glorious.’ So they struck his throat and killed him; and he, therefore, entered Paradise.”

  document.addEventListener("DOMContentLoaded", function () {

      threshold: 0.1

    const observer = new IntersectionObserver((entries, observer) => {
      entries.forEach(entry => {

          observer.unobserve(entry.target);
        }
      });
