    <section class="reveal-on-scroll">
      <h2 class="text-2xl sm:text-3xl font-semibold text-[#ECE8EF] pb-4 border-b border-[#332e29]/70">
        <span class="text-[#d6b278]">At-Tauhid</span> (The Oneness of Allah)
      </h2>

      <div class="mt-8 space-y-8 text-base sm:text-lg leading-8 text-[#ECE8EF]">

        <div>
          <p class="mb-4">Allah the Almighty said:</p>

          <blockquote class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-5 text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “And I (Allah) created not the Jinns and men except they should worship Me (Alone).” (51:56)
          </blockquote>
        </div>

        <div>
          <p class="mb-4">And He stated:</p>

          <blockquote class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-5 text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “And verily, We have sent among every Ummah (community, nation) a Messenger (proclaiming): ‘Worship Allah (Alone), and avoid (or keep away from) Taghut (all false deities etc. i.e. do not worship Taghut besides Allah).’ ” (16:36)
          </blockquote>
        </div>

        <div>
          <p class="mb-4">And He said:</p>

          <blockquote class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-5 text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “Worship Allah and join none with Him in worship.” (4:36)
          </blockquote>
        </div>

        <div>
          <p class="mb-4">And He said:</p>

          <blockquote class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-5 text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “And your Lord has decreed that you worship none but Him” (17:23)
          </blockquote>
        </div>

        <div>
          <p class="mb-4">Allah the Most Exalted said:</p>

          <blockquote class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-5 text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “It is those who believe (in the Oneness of Allah and worship none but Him Alone) and confuse not their belief with Zulm (wrong i.e. by worshipping other besides Allah), for them (only) there is security and they are guided ones.” (6:82)
          </blockquote>
        </div>

        <div>
          <p class="mb-4">Allah’s Messenger said:</p>

          <blockquote class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-5 space-y-5">
            <p>
              “Whoever testifies that there is nothing worthy of worship in truth (no God) except Allah Alone, Who is without (peer or) partner, and that Muhammad is His slave and Messenger, and that ‘Iesa (Jesus) is the slave of Allah, His Messenger, and His Word which He bestowed in Maryam (Mary) and a spirit (created) from Him, and that Paradise &amp; Hell-fire are realities, Allah will admit him into Paradise, whatever his deeds might be.”
            </p>

            <p>
              (Sahih Al-Bukhari, Hadith No. 3252)
            </p>
          </blockquote>
        </div>

        <div>
          <p class="mb-4">The Prophet said:</p>

          <blockquote class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-5">
            “Indeed Allah has forbidden for Hell the person who testifies: T here is nothing worthy of worship in truth (no true God) but Allah’, seeking thereby nothing but Allah’s Face.” (Al-Bukhari, Muslim).
          </blockquote>
        </div>

        <div>
          <p class="mb-4">At-Tirmidhi reports from Anas: He heard Allah’s Messenger saying:</p>

          <blockquote class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-5">
            “Allah the Most Exalted said: ‘O son of Adam, were you to come to Me with the world full of sins, and meet Me without making anything partner to Me {Shirk), I would come to you with a similar amount of forgiveness.’ ”
          </blockquote>
        </div>

      </div>
    </section>


    <section class="reveal-on-scroll">
      <h2 class="text-2xl sm:text-3xl font-semibold text-[#ECE8EF] pb-4 border-b border-[#332e29]/70">
        <span class="text-[#d6b278]">Fear of Shirk</span> (Polytheism)
      </h2>

      <div class="mt-8 space-y-8 text-base sm:text-lg leading-8 text-[#ECE8EF]">

        <div>
          <p class="mb-4">Allah the Almighty said:</p>

          <blockquote class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-5">
            “Verily, Allah forgives not that partners should be set up with Him in worship,

            but He forgives except that (anything else) to whom He pleases.”

            (4:48, 116)
          </blockquote>
        </div>

        <div>
          <p class="mb-4">Prophet Ibrahim said:</p>

          <blockquote class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-5">
            “And keep me and my sons away from worshipping idols.” (14:35)
          </blockquote>
        </div>

        <div>
          <p class="mb-4">Ibn Mas‘ud narrated that Prophet Muhammad said:</p>

          <blockquote class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-5">
            “Whoever dies while ascribing partners to Allah, enters the Hell-fire.” (Al-Bukhari)
          </blockquote>
        </div>

        <div>
          <p class="mb-4">Muslim reports from Jabir that Allah’s Messenger said:</p>

          <blockquote class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-5">
            “Whoever meets Allah (on the Day of Judgement) not having associated anyone with Him (in worship),

            shall enter Paradise; and whoever meets Him having committed Shirk in any way will enter the Hell-fire.”
          </blockquote>
        </div>

      </div>
    </section>


    <section class="reveal-on-scroll">
      <h2 class="text-xl sm:text-2xl font-semibold text-[#ECE8EF] leading-8">
        <span class="text-[#d6b278]">To wear a Ring, Twine, or anything similar to them for prevention or lifting of Harm or Affliction, is an act of Shirk</span>
      </h2>

      <div class="mt-8 text-base sm:text-lg leading-8 text-[#ECE8EF]">
        <p class="mb-4">The Prophet is reported to have said:</p>

        <blockquote class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-5">
          “Whoever wears a talisman has committed Shirk (polytheism).”
        </blockquote>
      </div>
    </section>


    <section class="reveal-on-scroll">
      <h2 class="text-xl sm:text-2xl font-semibold text-[#ECE8EF] leading-8">
        <span class="text-[#d6b278]">Whoever seeks Blessing through a Tree, a Stone, or the like</span>
      </h2>

      <div class="mt-8 text-base sm:text-lg leading-8 text-[#ECE8EF]">
        <p class="mb-4">Allah the Most Exalted said:</p>

        <blockquote class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-5">
          “Have you then considered al-Lat and Al-‘Uzza (the two idols of the pagan Arabs).

          And Manat (another idol of the pagan Arabs), the other third? (53:19,20)
        </blockquote>
      </div>
    </section>


    <section class="reveal-on-scroll">
      <h2 class="text-xl sm:text-2xl font-semibold text-[#ECE8EF] leading-8">
        <span class="text-[#d6b278]">Slaughtering for other than Allah</span>
      </h2>

      <div class="mt-8 space-y-8 text-base sm:text-lg leading-8 text-[#ECE8EF]">

        <div>
          <p class="mb-4">Allah the Exalted said:</p>

          <blockquote class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-5">
            “Say: Verily my prayer, my sacrifice, my living, and my dying are for Allah, the Lord of the worlds. He has no partner. And of this 1 have been commanded, and I am the first of the Muslims.” (6:162,163)
          </blockquote>
        </div>

        <div>
          <p class="mb-4">Ahmad reports that Tariq bin Shihab narrated that Allah’s Messenger said:</p>

          <blockquote class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-5 space-y-5">
            <p>
              “A man entered Paradise because of a fly, and a man entered Hell-fire because of a fly.”
            </p>

            <p>
              They (the Companions) asked, “How was that possible O Messenger of Allah?”
            </p>

            <p>
              He said, “Two men passed by the people who had an idol by which they would not allow anyone to pass without making sacrifice to it. They ordered one man to make a sacrifice. He said, T have nothing to present as an offering.’
            </p>

            <p>
              The people told him, ‘Sacrifice something, even if it be a fly.’ So he presented a fly (to their idol). They opened the way for him, and thus he entered the Hell-fire.
            </p>

            <p>
              They said to the other man, ‘Sacrifice something.’ He said, ‘I will never sacrifice anything to any other than Allah, Most Majestic and Glorious.’
            </p>

            <p>
              So they struck his throat and killed him; and he, therefore, entered Paradise.” (Ahmad)
            </p>
          </blockquote>
        </div>

      </div>
    </section>

            <span class="text-[#d6b278]">To vow to other than Allah</span> is an act of Shirk
          </p>

          <p class="text-base sm:text-lg leading-8 text-[#ECE8EF]">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278]">To seek Refuge in other than Allah</span> is a part of Shirk
          </p>
        </div>

        <div class="rounded-xl border border-[#332e29]/70 bg-[#1c1a18]/40 p-6 sm:col-span-2">
          <p class="text-base sm:text-lg leading-8 text-[#ECE8EF]">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278]">To seek help in other than Allah (Istighatha) or to invoke other than Him</span> is an act of Shirk
          </p>
        </div>

      </div>
    </section>


    <section class="reveal-on-scroll">
      <h2 class="text-xl sm:text-2xl font-semibold text-[#ECE8EF] leading-8">
        <span class="text-[#d6b278]">On Soothsayers and the like</span>
      </h2>

      <div class="mt-8 text-base sm:text-lg leading-8 text-[#ECE8EF]">
        <p class="mb-4">The Prophet said:</p>

        <blockquote class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-5">
          “Whoever visited a soothsayer and believed in his words, has denied the Revelation to Muhammad (i.e. Qur’an).” (Abu Dawud)
        </blockquote>
      </div>
    </section>
