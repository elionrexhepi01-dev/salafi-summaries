To possess true monotheism, one must fulfill each category through clear scriptural proof and authentic conviction.”

    <!-- PRINCIPLE I -->
    <section class="space-y-6 reveal-on-scroll">
      <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-[#332f2b] pb-4">
        <span class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Principle I</span>
        <h2 class="text-3xl font-medium text-white sm:text-4xl">
          The Inadequacy of Affirming Lordship Alone
        </h2>
      </div>

      <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF]">
        Acknowledging Allah as the sole Creator and Sustainer (<e>Tawḥīd ar-Rubūbīyah</e>) is a necessary foundation, yet it is insufficient on its own to enter Islam or secure salvation. Even the historical idolaters affirmed that Allah created them and governed the universe, yet this recognition did not make them believers.
      </p>

      <!-- Proof Card: Quranic Evidence -->
      <div class="bg-[#24211e] border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] p-6 rounded-r-lg space-y-3">
        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF] italic leading-relaxed">
          “Say: ‘Who provides for you from the heaven and the earth? Or who owns hearing and sight? And who brings out the living from the dead and brings out the dead from the living? And who disposes the affairs?’ They will say: ‘Allah.’ Say: ‘Will you not then fear Him?’”
        </p>
        <span class="text-sm font-mono text-[#d6b278] block">[Sūrah Yūnus 10:31]</span>
      </div>

      <!-- Scholarly Proof: Ibn Taymiyyah -->
      <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-6 rounded-lg border border-[#332f2b] space-y-3">
        <strong class="text-white font-medium text-lg block border-b border-[#332f2b] pb-2">
          Shaykh al-Islām Ibn Taymiyyah <span class="text-[#B3ADB9] text-sm font-normal">رَحِمَهُ ٱللَّٰهُ</span>
        </strong>
        <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#B3ADB9] italic">
          “The meaning of Tawḥīd is not merely Rubūbīyah—the belief that Allah alone created the world. Rather, one must also testify that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allah alone. For <e>al-Ilāh</e> is the One who is deified and worshipped, who alone deserves ultimate devotion.”
        </p>
      </div>

      <!-- Additional Quranic Evidence for Rububiyah -->
      <div class="pt-2 space-y-4">
        <h3 class="text-xl font-medium text-white">Scriptural Proofs for Tawḥīd ar-Rubūbīyah</h3>
        <div class="grid grid-cols-1 gap-4 sm:grid-cols-2">
          <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-4 rounded border border-[#332f2b] space-y-1">
            <p class="text-base text-[#ECE8EF] italic">“All praise and thanks belong to Allah, Lord of all the worlds.”</p>
            <span class="text-xs font-mono text-[#d6b278] block">[Sūrah al-Fātiḥah 1:2]</span>
          </div>
          <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-4 rounded border border-[#332f2b] space-y-1">
            <p class="text-base text-[#ECE8EF] italic">“Unquestionably, to Him belongs the creation and the command.”</p>
            <span class="text-xs font-mono text-[#d6b278] block">[Sūrah al-A‘rāf 7:54]</span>
          </div>
          <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-4 rounded border border-[#332f2b] space-y-1">
            <p class="text-base text-[#ECE8EF] italic">“Allah is the Creator of all things, and He is Trustee over all things.”</p>
            <span class="text-xs font-mono text-[#d6b278] block">[Sūrah az-Zumar 39:62]</span>
          </div>
          <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-4 rounded border border-[#332f2b] space-y-1">
            <p class="text-base text-[#ECE8EF] italic">“That is Allah, your Lord: so Blessed be Allah, Lord of the worlds.”</p>
            <span class="text-xs font-mono text-[#d6b278] block">[Sūrah Ghāfir 40:64]</span>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </section>

    <!-- PRINCIPLE II -->
    <section class="space-y-6 reveal-on-scroll">
      <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-[#332f2b] pb-4">
        <span class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Principle II</span>
        <h2 class="text-3xl font-medium text-white sm:text-4xl">
          Exclusive Divinity and Devotion (Tawḥīd al-Ulūhīyah)
        </h2>
      </div>

      <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#B3ADB9]">
        Linguistically and doctrinally, the name <e>Allah</e> signifies <e>al-Ma’lūh</e> and <e>al-Ma‘būd</e>—the One who is rightfully deified, revered, and worshipped. <e>Tawḥīd al-Ulūhīyah</e> requires directing every act of worship, both outwardly and inwardly, exclusively to Him.
      </p>

      <div class="grid grid-cols-1 gap-6 sm:grid-cols-2">
        <div class="bg-[#24211e] border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] p-6 rounded-r-lg space-y-2">
          <p class="text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic">
            “You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help.”
          </p>
          <span class="text-xs font-mono text-[#d6b278] block">[Sūrah al-Fātiḥah 1:5]</span>
        </div>

        <div class="bg-[#24211e] border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] p-6 rounded-r-lg space-y-2">
          <p class="text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic">
            “O mankind, worship your Lord, who created you and those before you, so that you may become righteous.”
          </p>
          <span class="text-xs font-mono text-[#d6b278] block">[Sūrah al-Baqarah 2:21]</span>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-6 rounded-lg border border-[#332f2b] space-y-2">
        <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic">
          “So worship Allah alone by offering religious deeds sincerely for His sake only.”
        </p>
        <span class="text-xs font-mono text-[#d6b278] block">[Sūrah az-Zumar 39:2]</span>
      </div>
    </section>

    <!-- PRINCIPLE III -->
    <section class="space-y-6 reveal-on-scroll">
      <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-[#332f2b] pb-4">
        <span class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Principle III</span>
        <h2 class="text-3xl font-medium text-white sm:text-4xl">
          Uniqueness in Divine Names and Attributes
        </h2>
      </div>

      <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#B3ADB9]">
        <e>Tawḥīd as-Sifāt</e> dictates affirming all names and attributes with which Allah has described Himself in the Qur’an and Sunnah, without distorting their meanings (<e>taḥrīf</e>), denying them (<e>ta‘ṭīl</e>), asking about their exact nature (<e>takyīf</e>), or likening them to creation (<e>tamthīl</e>).
      </p>

      <!-- Proof Card: Surah ash-Shura & al-Hashr -->
      <div class="bg-[#24211e] p-6 sm:p-8 rounded-lg border border-[#24211e] space-y-6">
        <div class="border-b border-[#332f2b] pb-4 space-y-2">
          <span class="text-xs font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">The Rule of Transcendence</span>
          <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF] italic leading-relaxed">
            “There is nothing like unto Him; and He is the All-Hearer, the All-Seer.”
          </p>
          <span class="text-xs font-mono text-[#d6b278] block">[Sūrah ash-Shūrā 42:11]</span>
        </div>

        <div class="space-y-2">
          <span class="text-xs font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Comprehensive Affirmation</span>
          <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#B3ADB9] leading-relaxed">
            “He is Allah, beside Whom there is no deity worthy of worship: the Knower of the unseen and the witnessed. He is the Most Gracious, the Most Merciful... The Sovereign, the Holy, the One Free from all defects, the Giver of security, the Watcher, the All-Mighty, the Compeller, the Supreme... To Him belong the Best Names.”
          </p>
          <span class="text-xs font-mono text-[#d6b278] block">[Sūrah al-Ḥashr 59:22-24]</span>
        </div>
      </div>
    </section>

    <!-- PRINCIPLE IV -->
    <section class="space-y-8 reveal-on-scroll">
      <div class="space-y-2 border-b border-[#332f2b] pb-4">
        <span class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Principle IV</span>
        <h2 class="text-3xl font-medium text-white sm:text-4xl">
          Historical Affirmation by the Early Salaf
        </h2>
      </div>

      <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#B3ADB9]">
        Contrary to the misconception that the tripartite categorization of Tawḥīd was an innovation of later scholars like Ibn Taymiyyah, explicit statements from the earliest classical authorities demonstrate that these distinctions were well-established centuries prior.
      </p>

      <!-- Numbered List: Ibn Battah's Framework -->
      <div class="bg-[#24211e] p-6 sm:p-8 rounded-lg border border-[#24211e] space-y-6">
        <div class="border-b border-[#332f2b] pb-4">
          <h3 class="text-xl font-medium text-white">Imām Ibn Baṭṭah al-‘Ukbarī <span class="text-sm font-normal text-[#B3ADB9]">(d. 387 AH)</span></h3>
          <p class="text-sm text-[#d6b278] font-mono mt-1">The Three Foundations of Faith (Īmān)</p>
        </div>

        <div class="space-y-6">
          <div class="flex items-start gap-4">
            <span class="text-2xl sm:text-3xl font-bold font-mono text-[#d6b278]">01.</span>
            <div class="space-y-1">
              <strong class="block text-lg text-white">Belief in Divinity (<e>Rabbānīyah</e>)</strong>
              <p class="text-base text-[#B3ADB9]">
                Affirming the Creator to be free from the path of the deniers (<e>Ahl at-Ta‘ṭīl</e>) who refuse to acknowledge a Supreme Maker.
              </p>
            </div>
          </div>

          <div class="flex items-start gap-4 border-t border-[#332f2b] pt-6">
            <span class="text-2xl sm:text-3xl font-bold font-mono text-[#d6b278]">02.</span>
            <div class="space-y-1">
              <strong class="block text-lg text-white">Belief in Oneness (<e>Waḥdānīyah</e>)</strong>
              <p class="text-base text-[#B3ADB9]">
                Singling Him out in worship to be free from the path of polytheism (<e>Shirk</e>), who affirm a Creator but associate partners with Him in devotion.
              </p>
            </div>
          </div>

          <div class="flex items-start gap-4 border-t border-[#332f2b] pt-6">
            <span class="text-2xl sm:text-3xl font-bold font-mono text-[#d6b278]">03.</span>
            <div class="space-y-1">
              <strong class="block text-lg text-white">Belief in Divine Attributes (<e>Ṣifāt</e>)</strong>
              <p class="text-base text-[#B3ADB9]">
                Firmly believing that He is described with the essential attributes revealed in His Book, such as Knowledge, Power, and Wisdom.
              </p>
            </div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Grid Callouts: Early Scholars -->
      <div class="pt-4 space-y-4">
        <h3 class="text-xl font-medium text-white">Statements of Early Pre-Ibn Taymiyyah Imāms</h3>

        <div class="grid grid-cols-1 gap-6 sm:grid-cols-2">

          <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-6 rounded-lg border border-[#332f2b] space-y-3">
            <span class="text-xs font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Imām Abū Ḥanīfah (d. 150 AH)</span>
            <p class="text-base text-[#B3ADB9] italic">
              “Allah is to be invoked upwards, not downwards, because lowness is not an attribute of <e>ar-Rubūbīyah</e> or <e>al-Ulūhīyah</e> in any respect.”
            </p>
          </div>

          <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-6 rounded-lg border border-[#332f2b] space-y-3">
            <span class="text-xs font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Ibn Jarīr aṭ-Ṭabarī (d. 310 AH)</span>
            <p class="text-base text-[#B3ADB9] italic">
              “Know that none is deserving of <e>al-Ulūhīyah</e> except Allah, who created all creation. Everything besides Him must submit to Him in worship through <e>ar-Rubūbīyah</e>.”
            </p>
          </div>

          <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-6 rounded-lg border border-[#332f2b] space-y-3">
            <span class="text-xs font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Imām aṭ-Ṭaḥāwī (d. 321 AH)</span>
            <p class="text-base text-[#B3ADB9] italic">
              “We say concerning the Tawḥīd of Allah: Allah is One without any partner, nothing is similar to Him, nothing renders Him incapable, and there is no deity besides Him.”
            </p>
          </div>

          <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-6 rounded-lg border border-[#332f2b] space-y-3">
            <span class="text-xs font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Ibn Abī Zayd al-Qayrawānī (d. 386 AH)</span>
            <p class="text-base text-[#B3ADB9] italic">
              “Faith requires professing with the tongue and believing in the heart that Allah is One God, with no equal. The true reality of His Attributes cannot be fully comprehended by thinkers.”
            </p>
          </div>

        </div>
