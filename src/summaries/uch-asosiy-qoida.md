      <h2 class="text-3xl font-normal leading-tight text-white sm:text-4xl border-b border-[#24211e] pb-5 text-center">
        Ikkinchi asosiy qoida
      </h2>

      <p class="text-lg leading-relaxed sm:text-xl">
        Sunnatdan dalil — Umar ibn al-Xattob roziyallohu anhudan rivoyat qilingan mashhur Jibril hadisidir. U zot shunday deganlar:
      </p>

      <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#332f2b] p-6 sm:p-8 rounded-lg space-y-6">

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          «Bir kuni biz Payg‘ambar sollallohu alayhi va sallam bilan birga o‘tirgan edik. Shu payt oldimizga kiyimlari juda oppoq, sochlari esa juda qop-qora bir kishi paydo bo‘ldi.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          Unda safardan kelganlik alomati ko‘rinmas, oramizdan hech kim uni tanimas edi. U Payg‘ambar sollallohu alayhi va sallamning oldilariga o‘tirib, tizzalarini u zotning tizzalariga tekkizdi va kaftlarini sonlariga qo‘yib dedi:
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          “Ey Muhammad, menga Islom haqida xabar bering”.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          U zot dedilar: “Islom — Allohdan o‘zga ibodatga loyiq iloh yo‘qligiga va Muhammad Allohning Rasuli ekaniga guvohlik berishing, namozni to‘kis ado etishing, zakot berishing, Ramazon ro‘zasini tutishing va agar bunga qodir bo‘lsang, Allohning Baytiga haj qilishingdir”.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          U kishi: “To‘g‘ri aytdingiz”, dedi.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          Biz uning Payg‘ambardan so‘rab, keyin u zotning javoblarini tasdiqlaganidan hayratlandik.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          So‘ng u: “Menga iymon haqida xabar bering”, dedi.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          U zot dedilar: “Iymon — Allohga, Uning farishtalariga, kitoblariga, payg‘ambarlariga, Oxirat kuniga va taqdirning yaxshisi-yomoniga ishonishingdir”.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          U kishi: “To‘g‘ri aytdingiz”, dedi.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          So‘ng: “Menga ihson haqida xabar bering”, dedi.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          U zot dedilar: “Ihson — Allohga go‘yo Uni ko‘rib turgandek ibodat qilishingdir. Garchi sen Uni ko‘rmasang ham, U seni albatta ko‘rib turadi”.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          Keyin u: “Menga Qiyomat soati haqida xabar bering”, dedi.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          U zot dedilar: “Bu haqda so‘ralayotgan kishi so‘rayotgan kishidan ko‘proq bilimga ega emas”.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          U kishi: “Unday bo‘lsa, uning alomatlari haqida xabar bering”, dedi.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          U zot dedilar: “Cho‘ri ayol o‘z xo‘jayinini tug‘ishi va yalangoyoq, kambag‘al hamda qo‘y boqadigan kishilarning baland binolar qurishda bir-birlari bilan musobaqalashishlarini ko‘rishingdir”.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          So‘ng u kishi ketdi. Biz esa bir muddat o‘tirib qoldik.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          Keyin u zot: “Ey Umar, savol beruvchi kim ekanini bilasanmi?” dedilar.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          Men: “Alloh va Uning Rasuli yaxshiroq biladi”, dedim.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          U zot dedilar: “Bu sizlarga diningizni o‘rgatish uchun kelgan Jibril edi”.
        </p>

      </div>
