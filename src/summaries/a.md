      <div class="border-b border-[#332f2b] pb-4">
        <h2 class="text-3xl font-semibold text-white sm:text-4xl">The Four Obligatory Matters</h2>
        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#B3ADB9] mt-2">It is obligatory upon us to learn four matters.</p>
      </div>

      <div class="grid grid-cols-1 gap-4 sm:grid-cols-2">
        <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-5 rounded-lg space-y-2">
          <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-bold text-xs uppercase tracking-wider">First</span>
          <h3 class="text-xl font-semibold text-white sm:text-2xl">Knowledge</h3>
          <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#B3ADB9]">Which means: awareness of Allaah, awareness of His Prophet, and awareness of the Religion of Islaam, based on evidences.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-5 rounded-lg space-y-2">
          <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-bold text-xs uppercase tracking-wider">Second</span>
          <h3 class="text-xl font-semibold text-white sm:text-2xl">Action</h3>
          <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#B3ADB9]">Acting on this.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-5 rounded-lg space-y-2">
          <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-bold text-xs uppercase tracking-wider">Third</span>
          <h3 class="text-xl font-semibold text-white sm:text-2xl">Call</h3>
          <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#B3ADB9]">Calling to it.</p>
        </div>
        <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-5 rounded-lg space-y-2">
          <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-bold text-xs uppercase tracking-wider">Fourth</span>
          <h3 class="text-xl font-semibold text-white sm:text-2xl">Patience</h3>
          <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#B3ADB9]">Patience with the harm that befalls due to it.</p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <div class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] bg-[#1c1a18] p-5 rounded-r-lg space-y-4">
        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF]">
          Al-Bukhaaree, may Allaah have mercy on him, said: <span class="font-medium text-white">“Chapter: Knowledge comes before speech and action.”</span>
        </p>
        <div class="space-y-2">
          <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#B3ADB9]">The proof for this is Allaah’s saying:</p>
          <blockquote class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#d6b278] font-medium italic border-l border-[#332f2b] pl-4 py-1">
            “So know that there is no deity worthy of worship except Allaah, and seek forgiveness for your sins.” <span class="text-[#B3ADB9] text-base not-italic">[Surah Muhammad: 19]</span>
          </blockquote>
          <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#B3ADB9] pt-1">So He began by mentioning knowledge before speech and action.</p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

  </section>

  <!-- The First Fundamental Principle -->
  <section class="space-y-6 reveal-on-scroll">
    <div class="border-b border-[#332f2b] pb-3">
      <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-mono text-xs sm:text-sm tracking-widest uppercase block mb-1">Principle I</span>
      <h2 class="text-3xl font-semibold text-white sm:text-4xl">The First Fundamental Principle</h2>
    </div>

    <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#332f2b] p-6 sm:p-8 rounded-xl space-y-6">
      <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-5 rounded-lg space-y-3">
        <h3 class="text-xl sm:text-2xl font-medium text-[#d6b278]">So if it is said: Who is your Lord?</h3>
        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF] leading-relaxed">
          Then say: My Lord is Allaah, the One who nurtured me and nurtured all of creation through His favors. And He is the One whom I worship, there being to me no (false) deity worshipped that is equal to Him.
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-5 rounded-lg space-y-3">
        <h3 class="text-xl sm:text-2xl font-medium text-[#d6b278]">So if it is said to you: How did you come to know of your Lord?</h3>
        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF] leading-relaxed">
          Then say: By way of His signs and His creations.
        </p>
      </div>

      <!-- Proof Card -->
      <div class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] bg-[#1c1a18] p-5 rounded-r-lg space-y-4">
        <span class="text-xs font-mono uppercase tracking-wider text-[#d6b278] block">Quranic Proof</span>
        <blockquote class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF] italic leading-relaxed">
          “O mankind! Worship your Lord who created you and those before you, so that you may be dutiful to Him. He is the One who made the earth a resting place for you, and the sky as a canopy, and sent down water from the sky and brought forth therewith fruits as a provision for you. So do no set up rivals with Allaah in worship knowingly.”
          <span class="text-[#d6b278] text-base font-normal block mt-2 not-italic">[Surah Al-Baqarah: 21-22]</span>
        </blockquote>
        <div class="border-t border-[#332f2b] pt-3">
          <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#B3ADB9]">
            Ibn Katheer, may Allaah have mercy on him, said: <span class="font-medium text-white">“The creator of these things is the One who truly deserves to be worshipped.”</span>
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Types of Worship -->
      <div class="pt-2 space-y-4">
        <h3 class="text-2xl font-semibold text-white sm:text-3xl">Types of Worship Commanded by Allaah</h3>
        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#B3ADB9] leading-relaxed">
          The types of worship that Allaah commanded, such as Islaam, Eemaan and Ihsaan, which includes: Supplication (Du’aa), Fear (Khawf), Hope (Rajaa), Reliance (Tawakkul), Longing (Raghbah) and Dreading (Rahbah), Submissiveness (Khushoo’), Awe (Khashyah), Repentance (Inaabah), Seeking Assistance (Isti’aanah), Seeking Refuge (Isti’aadhah), Asking for Help (Istighaathah), Offering Sacrifices (Dhabah), Making Oaths (Nadhar) and all of the other types of worship that Allaah commanded – all of these belong to Allaah, alone.
        </p>

        <div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-2 pt-2 sm:grid-cols-3 lg:grid-cols-4"><div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-3 rounded text-center text-sm sm:text-base text-[#ECE8EF]">Du’aa (Supplication)</div><div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-3 rounded text-center text-sm sm:text-base text-[#ECE8EF]">Khawf (Fear)</div><div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-3 rounded text-center text-sm sm:text-base text-[#ECE8EF]">Rajaa (Hope)</div><div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-3 rounded text-center text-sm sm:text-base text-[#ECE8EF]">Tawakkul (Reliance)</div><div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-3 rounded text-center text-sm sm:text-base text-[#ECE8EF]">Raghbah (Longing)</div><div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-3 rounded text-center text-sm sm:text-base text-[#ECE8EF]">Rahbah (Dreading)</div><div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-3 rounded text-center text-sm sm:text-base text-[#ECE8EF]">Khushoo’ (Submissiveness)</div><div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-3 rounded text-center text-sm sm:text-base text-[#ECE8EF]">Khashyah (Awe)</div><div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-3 rounded text-center text-sm sm:text-base text-[#ECE8EF]">Inaabah (Repentance)</div><div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-3 rounded text-center text-sm sm:text-base text-[#ECE8EF]">Isti’aanah (Seeking Assistance)</div><div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-3 rounded text-center text-sm sm:text-base text-[#ECE8EF]">Isti’aadhah (Seeking Refuge)</div><div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-3 rounded text-center text-sm sm:text-base text-[#ECE8EF]">Istighaathah (Asking for Help)</div><div text-center text-sm sm:text-base text-[#ECE8EF]">Nadhar (Making Oaths)</div></div>
      </div>
    </div>

  <!-- The Second Fundamental Principle -->
  <section class="space-y-6 reveal-on-scroll">
    <div class="border-b border-[#332f2b] pb-3">
      <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-mono text-xs sm:text-sm tracking-widest uppercase block mb-1">Principle II</span>
      <h2 class="text-3xl font-semibold text-white sm:text-4xl">The Second Fundamental Principle</h2>
    </div>

    <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#332f2b] p-6 sm:p-8 rounded-xl space-y-6">
      <div class="space-y-2">
        <span class="text-xs font-mono uppercase tracking-wider text-[#d6b278] block">Proof from the Sunnah</span>
        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF] leading-relaxed">
          The proof from the Sunnah is the famous hadeeth of Jibreel, which is reported from ‘Umar bin Al-Khattaab who said:
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-5 sm:p-6 rounded-lg space-y-6">
        <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#B3ADB9] italic leading-relaxed border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pl-4">
          “One day we were sitting with the Prophet when there appeared to us a man with extremely white garments and extremely black hair. No trace of journeying could be seen on him nor did any amongst us recognize him. Then he sat in front of the Prophet, lining up his knees with his knees and placing his palms upon his thighs, and said:”
        </p>

        <!-- Hadith Dialogue Sections -->
        <div class="space-y-6 border-t border-[#332f2b] pt-6">
          <!-- Islaam -->
          <div class="space-y-3">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-mono text-xs uppercase tracking-wider block">Question 1: Islaam</span>
            <blockquote class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic border-l border-[#332f2b] pl-3">
              ‘O Muhammad, inform me about Islaam.’
            </blockquote>
            <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF] leading-relaxed">
              So he said: <span class="font-medium text-white">‘It is that you testify that there is no deity that has the right to be worshipped except Allaah and that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allaah. And that you establish the prayer, give the Zakaat, fast during Ramadaan and perform the Hajj (pilgrimage) to (Allaah’s) House, if you are able to do it.’</span>
            </p>
            <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#B3ADB9]">
              He said: <span class="text-[#d6b278]">‘You have spoken truthfully.’</span> So we were amazed that he had asked him and then told him that he was truthful.
            </p>
          </div>

          <!-- Eemaan -->
          <div class="space-y-3 border-t border-[#332f2b] pt-6">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-mono text-xs uppercase tracking-wider block">Question 2: Eemaan</span>
            <blockquote class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic border-l border-[#332f2b] pl-3">
              Then he said: ‘Now inform me about Eemaan.’
            </blockquote>
            <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF] leading-relaxed">
              So he said: <span class="font-medium text-white">‘It is that you believe in Allaah, His angels, His (revealed) books, His messengers, the Last Day, and that you believe in Al-Qadar, the good of it and the bad of it.’</span>
            </p>
            <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#B3ADB9]">
              He said: <span class="text-[#d6b278]">‘You have spoken truthfully.’</span>
            </p>
          </div>

          <!-- Ihsaan -->
          <div class="space-y-3 border-t border-[#332f2b] pt-6">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-mono text-xs uppercase tracking-wider block">Question 3: Ihsaan</span>
            <blockquote class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic border-l border-[#332f2b] pl-3">
              ‘Now inform me about Ihsaan.’
            </blockquote>
            <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF] leading-relaxed">
              He said: <span class="font-medium text-white">‘It is that you worship Allaah as if you see Him, but even though you don’t see Him, He indeed sees you.’</span>
            </p>
          </div>

          <!-- The Hour -->
          <div class="space-y-3 border-t border-[#332f2b] pt-6">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-mono text-xs uppercase tracking-wider block">Question 4: The Hour</span>
            <blockquote class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic border-l border-[#332f2b] pl-3">
              He then said: ‘Now inform me about the (Final) Hour.’
            </blockquote>
            <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF] leading-relaxed">
              He said: <span class="font-medium text-white">‘The one who is being asked does not have any more knowledge of it than the one who is asking.’</span>
            </p>
          </div>

          <!-- Signs of the Hour -->
          <div class="space-y-3 border-t border-[#332f2b] pt-6">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-mono text-xs uppercase tracking-wider block">Question 5: Signs of the Hour</span>
            <blockquote class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic border-l border-[#332f2b] pl-3">
              He said: ‘So then inform me about its signs.’
            </blockquote>
            <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF] leading-relaxed">
              He said: <span class="font-medium text-white">‘It will be when the mother gives birth to her (female) master, when the barefooted, barren and lowly shepherds will compete with one another in constructing tall buildings.’</span>
            </p>
          </div>
        </div>

        <div class="border-t border-[#332f2b] pt-4 space-y-2 text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF]">
          <p>
            Then he left and we remained (seated) there for a while. Then he said: <span class="font-medium text-white">‘O ‘Umar, do you know who the questioner was?’</span> I said: ‘Allaah and His Messenger know best.’
          </p>
          <p class="text-[#d6b278] font-medium pt-1">
            He said: ‘That was Jibreel who came to you to teach you your Religion.’
          </p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

  </section>

      <h2 class="text-3xl font-semibold text-white sm:text-4xl">The Third Fundamental Principle</h2>
    </div>

    <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#332f2b] p-6 sm:p-8 rounded-xl space-y-6">
      <p class="text-[#d6b278] text-xl sm:text-2xl font-medium">
        Knowledge of your Prophet, Muhammad ﷺ.
      </p>

      <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-5 rounded-lg space-y-4">
        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF] leading-relaxed">
          This was his Religion – there was no good except that he directed his ummah towards it, and there was no evil except that he warned them against it.
        </p>

        <div class="grid grid-cols-1 gap-4 pt-2 sm:grid-cols-2">
          <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#332f2b] p-4 rounded-lg space-y-2">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-semibold text-xs uppercase tracking-wider">The Good Directed To</span>
            <p class="text-base font-medium text-white sm:text-lg">Tawheed and everything that Allaah loves and is pleased with.</p>
          </div>
          <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#332f2b] p-4 rounded-lg space-y-2">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-semibold text-xs uppercase tracking-wider">The Evil Warned Against</span>
            <p class="text-base font-medium text-white sm:text-lg">Shirk and everything that Allaah hates and rejects.</p>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Proof Card -->
      <div class="border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] bg-[#1c1a18] p-5 rounded-r-lg space-y-3">
        <span class="text-xs font-mono uppercase tracking-wider text-[#d6b278] block">Quranic Evidence</span>
        <blockquote class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF] italic leading-relaxed">
          “And We have indeed sent a messenger to every nation (saying): ‘Worship Allaah (alone) and avoid the false deities (Taaghoot).”
          <span class="text-[#d6b278] text-base font-normal block mt-2 not-italic">[Surah An-Nahl: 36]</span>
        </blockquote>
      </div>

      <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF] leading-relaxed">
        Allaah obligated all of His servants to disbelieve in the Taaghoot and believe in Allaah.
      </p>

      <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border border-[#332f2b] p-5 rounded-lg space-y-2">
        <span class="text-xs font-mono uppercase tracking-wider text-[#d6b278] block">Definition of Taaghoot</span>
        <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#B3ADB9]">
          Ibn Al-Qayyim, may Allaah have mercy on him, said: <span class="font-medium text-white">“The meaning of Taaghoot is someone or thing for whose sake a worshipper transgresses limits, such as those who are worshipped, followed or obeyed.”</span>
        </p>
      </div>
    </div>
