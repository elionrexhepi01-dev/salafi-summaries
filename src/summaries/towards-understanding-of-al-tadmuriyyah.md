    <!-- Section 1: The Core Quranic Principles -->
    <section class="space-y-6 reveal-on-scroll">
      <div class="text-center space-y-2 border-b border-[#24211e] pb-4">
        <p class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Principle I</p>
        <h2 class="text-2xl font-medium tracking-wide text-white sm:text-3xl font-serif-sub">
          The Foundations of Affirmation & Negation
        </h2>
      </div>

      <p class="text-base leading-relaxed sm:text-lg">
        The methodology of the Qur’an in establishing belief in Allah relies upon two foundational pillars: <strong class="font-medium text-white">Affirmation (Ithbāt)</strong> of attributes of perfection and <strong class="font-medium text-white">Negation (Nafy)</strong> of all attributes of deficiency.
      </p>

      <!-- Evidences Card -->
      <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#24211e] p-6 rounded-lg space-y-6">
        <span class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278] block border-b border-[#332f2b] pb-2">
          Foundational Qur'anic Evidence
        </span>

        <!-- Proof 1 -->
        <div class="space-y-3">
          <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic leading-relaxed pl-3 border-l-2 border-[#d6b278]">
            “Allah! None has the right to be worshipped but He, the Ever-Living, the One Who sustains and protects all that exists. Neither slumber nor sleep overtakes Him.”
            <span class="text-sm text-[#d6b278] font-normal block mt-1">[Al-Baqarah 2:255]</span>
          </p>
          <ul class="text-sm space-y-1 pl-4 list-disc text-[#B3ADB9]">
            <li><strong class="text-white">“None has the right to be worshipped but He”:</strong> Affirms the Oneness of Allah and serves as evidence of His ultimate perfection.</li>
            <li><strong class="text-white">“Neither slumber nor sleep overtakes Him”:</strong> Negates imperfection, which inherently implies the fullness and absolute nature of His life and maintenance.</li>
          </ul>
        </div>

        <!-- Proof 2 -->
        <div class="space-y-3 border-t border-[#332f2b] pt-4">
          <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic leading-relaxed pl-3 border-l-2 border-[#d6b278]">
            “There is nothing like unto Him, and He is the All-Hearer, the All-Seer.”
            <span class="text-sm text-[#d6b278] font-normal block mt-1">[Ash-Shūrā 42:11]</span>
          </p>
          <ul class="text-sm space-y-1 pl-4 list-disc text-[#B3ADB9]">
            <li><strong class="text-white">“There is nothing like unto Him”:</strong> Negates resemblance to creation (<e>Tamthīl</e>) and nullifies anthropomorphic methodologies.</li>
            <li><strong class="text-white">“He is the All-Hearer, the All-Seer”:</strong> Affirms His divine Names and Attributes against divestment (<e>Ta‘ṭīl</e>) and distortion (<e>Taḥrīf</e>).</li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- The Four Golden Boundaries -->
      <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border-l-4 border-[#d6b278] p-5 rounded-r-lg space-y-2">
        <h3 class="text-base font-medium text-white sm:text-lg">The Path of Ahl al-Sunnah: The Four Boundaries</h3>
        <p class="text-sm text-[#B3ADB9] leading-relaxed">
          We must affirm for Allah what He has affirmed for Himself and deny what He has denied about Himself, strictly avoiding four pitfalls:
        </p>
        <div class="grid grid-cols-2 gap-3 pt-2 text-center sm:grid-cols-4">
          <div class="bg-[#24211e] p-2 rounded text-xs text-[#ECE8EF] border border-[#332f2b]">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-bold block mb-1">No Taḥrīf</span> Distortion of Meaning
          </div>
          <div class="bg-[#24211e] p-2 rounded text-xs text-[#ECE8EF] border border-[#332f2b]">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-bold block mb-1">No Ta‘ṭīl</span> Denial / Divestment
          </div>
          <div class="bg-[#24211e] p-2 rounded text-xs text-[#ECE8EF] border border-[#332f2b]">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-bold block mb-1">No Takyīf</span> Speculating "How"
          </div>
          <div class="bg-[#24211e] p-2 rounded text-xs text-[#ECE8EF] border border-[#332f2b]">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-bold block mb-1">No Tamthīl</span> Likening to Creation
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </section>

    <!-- Section 2: Affirmative vs. Negative Attributes -->
    <section class="space-y-6 reveal-on-scroll">
      <div class="text-center space-y-2 border-b border-[#24211e] pb-4">
        <p class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Principle II</p>
        <h2 class="text-2xl font-medium tracking-wide text-white sm:text-3xl font-serif-sub">
          Affirmative & Negative Attributes
        </h2>
      </div>

      <p class="text-base leading-relaxed sm:text-lg">
        Every negation in the Qur’an regarding Allah is not merely a non-existence of a quality; rather, it implies the <strong class="text-white">affirmation of its perfect opposite</strong>.
      </p>

      <div class="grid grid-cols-1 gap-4 md:grid-cols-2">
        <!-- Affirmative Attributes -->
        <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#24211e] p-5 rounded-lg space-y-3">
          <span class="text-xs font-mono uppercase tracking-wider text-[#d6b278] block">Affirmative Attributes (Aṣ-Ṣifāt ath-Thubūtiyyah)</span>
          <p class="text-xs text-[#B3ADB9]">All attributes affirmed by Allah are inherently perfect with zero deficiency.</p>
          <div class="flex flex-wrap gap-2 pt-2">
            <span class="bg-[#1c1a18] px-2.5 py-1 rounded text-xs text-[#ECE8EF] border border-[#332f2b]">Life (Ḥayāt)</span>
            <span class="bg-[#1c1a18] px-2.5 py-1 rounded text-xs text-[#ECE8EF] border border-[#332f2b]">Power (Qudrah)</span>
            <span class="bg-[#1c1a18] px-2.5 py-1 rounded text-xs text-[#ECE8EF] border border-[#332f2b]">Wisdom (Ḥikmah)</span>
            <span class="bg-[#1c1a18] px-2.5 py-1 rounded text-xs text-[#ECE8EF] border border-[#332f2b]">Knowledge (‘Ilm)</span>
            <span class="bg-[#1c1a18] px-2.5 py-1 rounded text-xs text-[#ECE8EF] border border-[#332f2b]">Honor (‘Izzah)</span>
            <span class="bg-[#1c1a18] px-2.5 py-1 rounded text-xs text-[#ECE8EF] border border-[#332f2b]">Speech (Kalām)</span>
          </div>
        </div>

        <!-- Negated Attributes & Their Meaning -->
        <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#24211e] p-5 rounded-lg space-y-3">
          <span class="text-xs font-mono uppercase tracking-wider text-[#d6b278] block">Negated Attributes (Aṣ-Ṣifāt as-Salbiyyah)</span>
          <p class="text-xs text-[#B3ADB9]">Negation of flaw implies perfect affirmation of the opposite attribute:</p>
          <ul class="text-xs space-y-2 pt-1 text-[#ECE8EF]">
            <li class="flex items-start justify-between border-b border-[#332f2b] pb-1">
              <span>Negation of <strong class="text-white">Oppression</strong></span>
              <span class="text-[#d6b278]">➔ Perfect Justice</span>
            </li>
            <li class="flex items-start justify-between border-b border-[#332f2b] pb-1">
              <span>Negation of <strong class="text-white">Fatigue / Weariness</strong></span>
              <span class="text-[#d6b278]">➔ Perfect Power</span>
            </li>
            <li class="flex items-start justify-between border-b border-[#332f2b] pb-1">
              <span>Negation of <strong class="text-white">Slumber & Sleep</strong></span>
              <span class="text-[#d6b278]">➔ Absolute Life & Sustaining Power</span>
            </li>
            <li class="flex items-start justify-between">
              <span>Negation of <strong class="text-white">Death</strong></span>
              <span class="text-[#d6b278]">➔ Everlasting Existence</span>
            </li>
          </ul>
        </div>
      </div>
    </section>

    <!-- Section 3: Analysis of Deviated Sects -->
    <section class="space-y-6 reveal-on-scroll">
      <div class="text-center space-y-2 border-b border-[#24211e] pb-4">
        <p class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Principle III</p>
        <h2 class="text-2xl font-medium tracking-wide text-white sm:text-3xl font-serif-sub">
          Categorization of Historical Deviations
        </h2>
      </div>

      <p class="text-base leading-relaxed sm:text-lg">
        Those who departed from the path of the Prophets fell into imbalance by exaggerating either in affirmation or in negation:
      </p>

      <div class="space-y-4">
        <!-- Mumaththila -->
        <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#24211e] p-5 rounded-lg">
          <h3 class="flex items-center justify-between mb-1 text-base font-medium text-white">
            <span>1. Al-Mumaththila <span class="text-xs text-[#B3ADB9] font-normal">(Anthropomorphists)</span></span>
            <span class="text-xs font-mono text-[#d6b278] bg-[#1c1a18] px-2 py-0.5 rounded">Excessive Affirmation</span>
          </h3>
          <p class="text-sm text-[#B3ADB9]">
            Exaggerated in affirmation to the point of comparing Allah’s attributes to creation, neglecting the essential negation of resemblance (<e>Tamthīl</e>).
          </p>
        </div>

        <!-- Mu'attilah Overview -->
        <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#24211e] p-5 rounded-lg space-y-3">
          <h3 class="flex items-center justify-between text-base font-medium text-white">
            <span>2. Al-Mu‘aṭṭilah <span class="text-xs text-[#B3ADB9] font-normal">(Deniers & Divesters)</span></span>
            <span class="text-xs font-mono text-[#d6b278] bg-[#1c1a18] px-2 py-0.5 rounded">Excessive Negation</span>
          </h3>
          <p class="text-sm text-[#B3ADB9]">
            Exaggerated in negation out of fear of likening Allah to creation, thereby divesting Allah of His actual Names and Attributes. This main category branches into various spectrums:
          </p>

          <div class="space-y-2 pt-2 border-t border-[#332f2b] text-xs">
            <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-3 rounded">
              <strong class="block mb-1 text-white">Al-Ashā‘irah (Ash'arites):</strong>
              <p class="text-[#B3ADB9]">Affirm the Names and a subset of attributes, but reject or reinterpret (<e>Ta’wīl</e>) the rest, believing their intellect contradicts literal textual evidence.</p>
            </div>

            <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-3 rounded">
              <strong class="block mb-1 text-white">Al-Mu‘tazilah (Mu'tazilites):</strong>
              <p class="text-[#B3ADB9]">Affirm Names merely as proper nouns devoid of entailed attributes (e.g., claiming He is "Knower without knowledge" or "Powerful without power").</p>
            </div>

            <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-3 rounded">
              <strong class="block mb-1 text-white">Extremists (Jahmiyyah, Al-Qarāmiṭah, Al-Bāṭiniyyah):</strong>
              <p class="text-[#B3ADB9]">Deny both Names and Attributes. Some fall into ultimate paradoxes by denying both affirmation and negation simultaneously—stating He is "neither existing nor non-existing, neither alive nor dead."</p>
            </div>
          </div>
        </div>
      </div>
    </section>

    <!-- Section 4: Consequences of Deviation -->

      <div class="text-center space-y-2 border-b border-[#24211e] pb-4">
        <p class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Principle IV</p>
        <h2 class="text-2xl font-medium tracking-wide text-white sm:text-3xl font-serif-sub">
          Dangers & Pitfalls of Theological Deviation
        </h2>
      </div>

      <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#24211e] p-6 rounded-lg">
        <ul class="space-y-3 text-sm text-[#ECE8EF]">
          <li class="flex items-start gap-3">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-mono font-bold">01.</span>
            <span><strong class="text-white">Departure from the Salaf:</strong> Deviation from the balanced, scriptural understanding of the pious predecessors (<e>Al-Salaf al-Ṣāliḥ</e>).</span>
          </li>
          <li class="flex items-start gap-3 border-t border-[#332f2b] pt-3">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-mono font-bold">02.</span>
            <span><strong class="text-white">Textual Distortion:</strong> Altering and distorting revealed texts (<e>Taḥrīf</e>) from their clear meanings.</span>
          </li>
          <li class="flex items-start gap-3 border-t border-[#332f2b] pt-3">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-mono font-bold">03.</span>
            <span><strong class="text-white">Imposing False Meanings:</strong> Misinterpreting divine revelations to fit speculative philosophical frameworks.</span>
          </li>
          <li class="flex items-start gap-3 border-t border-[#332f2b] pt-3">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-mono font-bold">04.</span>
            <span><strong class="text-white">Stripping Divine Perfection:</strong> Denying the lofty attributes of perfection that Allah established for Himself in scripture.</span>
          </li>
          <li class="flex items-start gap-3 border-t border-[#332f2b] pt-3">
            <span class="text-[#d6b278] font-mono font-bold">05.</span>
            <span><strong class="text-white">Internal Contradiction:</strong> Falling into self-contradictory logic when attempting to justify what is affirmed versus what is denied.</span>
          </li>
        </ul>
      </div>
    </section>
