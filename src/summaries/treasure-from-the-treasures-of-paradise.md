          Linguistic Depths & Scholarly Explanations
        </h2>
      </div>

      <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF]">
        The revered statement <e>“Lā Ḥawla wa lā Quwwata illā Billāh”</e> carries a precise linguistic framework. <e>Ḥawl</e> signifies movement or transformation from one state to another, while <e>Quwwah</e> denotes inherent strength.
      </p>

      <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#B3ADB9]">
        Thus, the statement affirms that a servant possesses neither the power to change their condition nor the strength to execute any affair except through the divine assistance, enabling grace (<e>tawfīq</e>), and guidance of Allah.
      </p>

      <!-- Grid Callout for Salaf Statements -->
      <div class="grid grid-cols-1 gap-6 sm:grid-cols-2">

        <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-6 rounded-lg border border-[#332f2b] space-y-2">
          <span class="text-xs font-mono text-[#d6b278] uppercase">‘Abdullāh ibn ‘Abbās <span class="text-[#B3ADB9]">رَضِيَ ٱللَّٰهُ عَنْهُ</span></span>
          <p class="text-base text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “We have no power to act obediently except by Allah, nor do we have strength to abandon sin except by Allah.”
          </p>
        </div>

        <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-6 rounded-lg border border-[#332f2b] space-y-2">
          <span class="text-xs font-mono text-[#d6b278] uppercase">‘Abdullāh ibn Mas‘ūd <span class="text-[#B3ADB9]">رَضِيَ ٱللَّٰهُ عَنْهُ</span></span>
          <p class="text-base text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “There is no strength to refrain from sinning against Allah except through His protection, and no power to obey Him except through His help.”
          </p>
        </div>

        <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-6 rounded-lg border border-[#332f2b] space-y-2">
          <span class="text-xs font-mono text-[#d6b278] uppercase">‘Alī ibn Abī Ṭālib <span class="text-[#B3ADB9]">رَضِيَ ٱللَّٰهُ عَنْهُ</span></span>
          <p class="text-base text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “We do not own anything alongside Allah or instead of Him; we only own what He permits us to possess, and it ultimately belongs entirely to Him.”
          </p>
        </div>

        <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-6 rounded-lg border border-[#332f2b] space-y-2">
          <span class="text-xs font-mono text-[#d6b278] uppercase">Zuhayr ibn Muḥammad <span class="text-[#B3ADB9]">رَحِمَهُ ٱللَّٰهُ</span></span>
          <p class="text-base text-[#ECE8EF]">
            “You do not obtain what you love except by Allah, and you do not avoid what you hate except through the help of Allah.”
          </p>
        </div>

      </div>

            <p class="text-base text-[#B3ADB9]">
              Explicitly identified by the Prophet ﷺ as one of the enduring righteous deeds mentioned in Sūrah al-Kahf [18:46], alongside <e>Allāhu Akbar</e>, <e>Lā ilāha illallāh</e>, <e>SubḥānAllāh</e>, and <e>Alḥamdulillāh</e>.

          <span class="text-2xl sm:text-3xl font-bold font-mono text-[#d6b278]">02.</span>
          <div class="space-y-1">
            <h3 class="text-lg font-medium text-white sm:text-xl">A Treasure Beneath the Divine Throne</h3>
            <p class="text-base text-[#B3ADB9]">
              The Prophet ﷺ stated: “Say <e>Lā Ḥawla wa lā Quwwata illā Billāh</e>, for it is a treasure from below the Throne.” Imam al-Nawawī <span class="text-[#B3ADB9]">رَحِمَهُ ٱللَّٰهُ</span> clarified that “treasure” refers to a priceless, stored celestial reward.

          <span class="text-2xl sm:text-3xl font-bold font-mono text-[#d6b278]">03.</span>
          <div class="space-y-1">
            <h3 class="text-lg font-medium text-white sm:text-xl">A Gateway to Paradise</h3>
            <p class="text-base text-[#B3ADB9]">
              The Messenger ﷺ offered to guide his companions directly to one of the specific gates of Paradise by pointing to the constant utterance of this phrase.
            </p>
          </div>
        </div>

        <div class="flex items-start gap-4 border-t border-[#332f2b] pt-6">
          <span class="text-2xl sm:text-3xl font-bold font-mono text-[#d6b278]">04.</span>
          <div class="space-y-1">
            <h3 class="text-lg font-medium text-white sm:text-xl">Divine Affirmation of Truthfulness</h3>
            <p class="text-base text-[#B3ADB9]">
              When a servant proclaims this phrase alongside Tawḥīd, Allah affirms from above the heavens: “My servant has spoken the truth; there is no deity worthy of worship except Me, and there is no power or might except through Me.”
            </p>
          </div>
        </div>

        <h2 class="text-3xl font-medium text-white sm:text-4xl">
          The Theological Foundations of Absolute Reliance
        </h2>
      </div>

      <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF]">
        Beyond its verbal simplicity, this statement encapsulates the core tenets of Islamic creed (<e>‘Aqīdah</e>). It is a proclamation of complete submission (<e>istiṣlām</e>), the entrusting of affairs (<e>tafwīḍ</e>), and total disavowal of personal power or independent capacity.
      </p>

      <!-- Theological Dimensions Grid -->
      <div class="grid grid-cols-1 gap-6 sm:grid-cols-2">

        <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-6 rounded-lg border border-[#332f2b] space-y-3">
          <span class="text-xs font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Sincerity in Seeking Help</span>
          <h3 class="text-xl font-medium text-white">Ikhlāṣ in Isti‘ānah</h3>
          <p class="text-base text-[#B3ADB9]">
            Just as <e>Lā ilāha illallāh</e> establishes sincerity in worship, <e>Lā Ḥawla wa lā Quwwata illā Billāh</e> establishes absolute sincerity in seeking divine assistance.
          </p>
        </div>

        <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-6 rounded-lg border border-[#332f2b] space-y-3">
          <span class="text-xs font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Monotheism of Lordship</span>
          <h3 class="text-xl font-medium text-white">Affirmation of Rubūbiyyah</h3>
          <p class="text-base text-[#B3ADB9]">
            It acknowledges Allah as the sole Creator and Sovereign Controller of the universe, who decrees all matters according to His ultimate Wisdom.
          </p>
        </div>

        <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-6 rounded-lg border border-[#332f2b] space-y-3">
          <span class="text-xs font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Divine Attributes</span>
          <h3 class="text-xl font-medium text-white">Asmā’ wa’ṣ-Ṣifāt & Human Need</h3>
          <p class="text-base text-[#B3ADB9]">
            The phrase affirms that Allah is entirely free of needs (<e>al-Ghanī</e>), while creation remains intrinsically needy and poor (<e>al-Faqīr</e>) before Him in every instant.
          </p>
        </div>

        <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] p-6 rounded-lg border border-[#332f2b] space-y-3">
          <span class="text-xs font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Predestination</span>
          <h3 class="text-xl font-medium text-white">Belief in Qaḍā’ wa’l-Qadar</h3>
          <p class="text-base text-[#B3ADB9]">
            It embeds firm belief in the Divine Will (<e>Mashī’ah</e>): whatever Allah wills must inevitably occur, and whatever He does not will can never come to pass.
          </p>
        </div>

      </div>

      <!-- Synthesis Card -->
      <div class="bg-[#24211e] border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] p-6 sm:p-8 rounded-r-lg space-y-3">
        <h3 class="text-xl font-medium text-white">Synthesis</h3>
        <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#B3ADB9]">
          <e>Lā Ḥawla wa lā Quwwata illā Billāh</e> is far more than a routine utterance; it is the soul of supplication and the practical realization of servitude. By constantly acknowledging complete helplessness before God, the believer receives divine strength, divine support, and an unshakable path toward Paradise.
        </p>
      </div>
