      <h2 class="text-3xl font-normal leading-tight text-white sm:text-4xl border-b border-[#24211e] pb-5 text-center">
        The Second Fundamental Principle
      </h2>

      <p class="text-lg leading-relaxed sm:text-xl">
        The proof from the Sunnah is the famous hadeeth of Jibreel, which is reported from ‘Umar bin Al-Khattaab who said:
      </p>

      <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#332f2b] p-6 sm:p-8 rounded-lg space-y-6">

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          “One day we were sitting with the Prophet when there appeared to us a man with extremely white garments and extremely black hair.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          No trace of journeying could be seen on him nor did any amongst us recognize him. Then he sat in front of the Prophet, lining up his knees with his ﷺ knees and placing his palms upon his thighs, and said:
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          ‘O Muhammad, inform me about Islam.’
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          So he said: ‘It is that you testify that there is no deity that has the right to be worshipped except Allaah and that Muhammad is the Messenger of Allaah. And that you establish the prayer, give the Zakaat, fast during Ramadaan and perform the Hajj (pilgrimage) to (Allaah’s) House, if you are able to do it.’
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          He said: ‘You have spoken truthfully.’
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          So we were amazed that he had asked him and then told him that he was truthful.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          Then he said: ‘Now inform me about Iman.’
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          So he said: ‘It is that you believe in Allaah, His angels, His (revealed) books, His messengers, the Last Day, and that you believe in Al-Qadar, the good of it and the bad of it.’
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          He said: ‘You have spoken truthfully.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          Now inform me about Ihsan.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          He said: ‘It is that you worship Allaah as if you see Him, but even though you don’t see Him, He indeed sees you.’
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          He then said: ‘Now inform me about the (Final) Hour.’
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          He said: ‘The one who is being asked does not have any more knowledge of it than the one who is asking.’
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          He said: ‘So then inform me about its signs.’
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          He said: ‘It will be when the mother gives birth to her (female) master, when the barefooted, barren and lowly shepherds will compete with one another in constructing tall buildings.’
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          Then he left and we remained (seated) there for a while.
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          Then he said: ‘O ‘Umar, do you know who the questioner was?’
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          I said: ‘Allaah and His Messenger know best.’
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          He said: ‘That was Jibreel who came to you to teach you your Religion.’”
        </p>

      </div>

    </section>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          Knowledge of your Prophet, Muhammad ﷺ.
        </p>
      </div>

      <p class="text-lg leading-relaxed sm:text-xl">
        This was his Religion – there was no good except that he directed his ummah towards it, and there was no evil except that he warned them against it.
      </p>

      <div class="grid grid-cols-1 gap-5 md:grid-cols-2">

        <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#332f2b] p-6 rounded-lg">
          <p class="text-lg leading-relaxed sm:text-xl">
            The good that he directed his ummah to was: <span class="text-white">Tawheed</span> and everything that Allaah loves and is pleased with.
          </p>
        </div>
        <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#332f2b] p-6 rounded-lg">
          <p class="text-lg leading-relaxed sm:text-xl">
            The evil that he warned his ummah about was: <span class="text-white">Shirk</span> and everything that Allaah hates and rejects.
          </p>
        </div>

      </div>

      <div class="bg-[#24211e] border-l-4 border-[#d6b278] p-6 sm:p-8 rounded-r-lg space-y-5">

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          “And We have indeed sent a messenger to every nation (saying):
        </p>

        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl leading-relaxed text-[#ECE8EF]">
          ‘Worship Allaah (alone) and avoid the false deities (Taaghoot).”
        </p>

        <p class="text-sm sm:text-base text-[#d6b278]">
          [Surah An-Nahl: 36]
        </p>

      </div>

      <p class="text-lg leading-relaxed sm:text-xl">
        Allaah obligated all of His servants to disbelieve in the Taaghoot and believe in Allaah.
      </p>

      <div class="bg-[#1c1a18] border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] p-6 sm:p-7 rounded-r-lg">
        <p class="text-lg leading-relaxed sm:text-xl">
          Ibn Al-Qayyim, may Allaah have mercy on him, said: “The meaning of Taaghoot is someone or thing for whose sake a worshipper transgresses limits, such as those who are worshipped, followed or obeyed.”
        </p>
      </div>
