    <div class="space-y-6 reveal-on-scroll">
      <div class="bg-[#24211e] border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] p-6 rounded-r-lg space-y-3">
        <p class="text-lg sm:text-xl text-[#ECE8EF] italic leading-relaxed font-sans-body">
          “Tawḥīd ar-Rubūbiyyah alone is not enough to enter Islām.”
        </p>
      </div>

      <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#24211e] p-6 rounded-lg space-y-3">
        <p class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Insight from Shaykh al-Islām Ibn Taymiyyah (رَحِمَهُ ٱللَّٰهُ)</p>
        <blockquote class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic leading-relaxed pl-2 border-none">
          “The meaning of Tawḥīd is not Rubūbiyyah only (i.e., the belief that Allah alone is the Creator of the world); rather, he must also testify that there is no Ilāh except Allah alone. And the Ilāh is the One who is deified and worshipped, and He is deserving of worship.”
        </blockquote>
      </div>
    </div>

    <!-- Scriptural Evidences for the Three Categories -->
    <div class="space-y-8 reveal-on-scroll">
      <div class="text-center space-y-2 border-b border-[#24211e] pb-4">
        <p class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Qur’ānic Proofs</p>
        <h2 class="text-3xl font-medium tracking-wide text-white uppercase font-serif-sub">
          Evidences from the Book of Allah
        </h2>
      </div>

      <!-- Category 1: Ruboobiyyah -->
      <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#24211e] p-6 rounded-lg space-y-3">
        <div class="flex items-center justify-between border-b border-[#24211e] pb-2">
          <span class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Category One</span>
          <span class="text-sm text-[#B3ADB9]">Tawḥīd ar-Rubūbiyyah</span>
        </div>
        <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic leading-relaxed pl-2 border-l-2 border-[#d6b278]">
          “Say: ‘Who provides for you from the sky and the earth? Or who owns hearing and sight? And who brings out the living from the dead and brings out the dead from the living? And who disposes the affairs?’ They will say: ‘Allāh.’” <span class="text-sm text-[#d6b278]">[Qur’ān 10:31]</span>
        </p>
        <div class="text-sm sm:text-base text-[#B3ADB9] pt-2 space-y-1">
          <p>• “All the praises and thanks be to Allāh, the Lord of the ‘Ālamīn.” <span class="text-[#d6b278]">[1:2]</span></p>
          <p>• “Surely, His is the Creation and Commandment.” <span class="text-[#d6b278]">[7:54]</span></p>
          <p>• “Allāh is the Creator of all things, and He is the Wakīl over all things.” <span class="text-[#d6b278]">[39:62]</span></p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Category 2: Uluhiyyah -->
      <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#24211e] p-6 rounded-lg space-y-3">
        <div class="flex items-center justify-between border-b border-[#24211e] pb-2">
          <span class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Category Two</span>
          <span class="text-sm text-[#B3ADB9]">Tawḥīd al-Ulūhiyyah</span>
        </div>
        <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic leading-relaxed pl-2 border-l-2 border-[#d6b278]">
          “O mankind! Worship your Lord (Allāh), Who created you and those who were before you so that you may become Al-Muttaqūn.” <span class="text-sm text-[#d6b278]">[Qur’ān 2:21]</span>
        </p>
        <div class="text-sm sm:text-base text-[#B3ADB9] pt-2 space-y-1">
          <p>• “All the praises and thanks be to Allāh” — For Allāh means <em>al-Ma’lūh</em> (the Deified One) and <em>al-Ma‘būd</em> (the Worshipped One). <span class="text-[#d6b278]">[1:2]</span></p>
          <p>• “You (Alone) we worship, and You (Alone) we ask for help.” <span class="text-[#d6b278]">[1:5]</span></p>
          <p>• “So worship Allāh (Alone) by doing religious deeds sincerely for Allāh’s sake only.” <span class="text-[#d6b278]">[39:2]</span></p>
        </div>
      </div>

      <!-- Category 3: Asmaa was Sifat -->
      <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#24211e] p-6 rounded-lg space-y-3">
        <div class="flex items-center justify-between border-b border-[#24211e] pb-2">
          <span class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Category Three</span>
          <span class="text-sm text-[#B3ADB9]">Tawḥīd al-Asmā’ wa al-Ṣifāt</span>
        </div>
        <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic leading-relaxed pl-2 border-l-2 border-[#d6b278]">
          “There is nothing like Him; and He is the All-Hearer, the All-Seer.” <span class="text-sm text-[#d6b278]">[Qur’ān 42:11]</span>
        </p>
        <div class="text-sm sm:text-base text-[#B3ADB9] pt-2 space-y-1">
          <p>• “To Him belong the Best Names.” <span class="text-[#d6b278]">[20:8, 17:110]</span></p>
          <p>• “Do you know of any who is similar to Him?” <span class="text-[#d6b278]">[19:65]</span></p>
          <p>• “He is Allāh, beside Whom Lā ilāha illā Huwa... the King, the Holy, the One Free from all defects, the Giver of security...” <span class="text-[#d6b278]">[59:22-24]</span></p>
        </div>
      </div>
    </div>

        <h2 class="text-3xl font-medium tracking-wide text-white uppercase font-serif-sub">
          Statements of the Early Salaf
        </h2>
      </div>

      <!-- Abu Hanifah & At-Tabari -->
      <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#24211e] p-6 rounded-lg space-y-3">
        <div class="flex items-center justify-between border-b border-[#24211e] pb-2">
          <span class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Imām Abū Ḥanīfah & Ibn Jarīr at-Ṭabarī</span>
          <span class="text-sm text-[#B3ADB9]">Early Affirmations</span>
        </div>
        <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic leading-relaxed pl-2 border-l-2 border-[#d6b278]">
          <strong>Abū Ḥanīfah:</strong> “And Allah is to be called upon upwards, not downwards, because lowness is not from the description of Ar-Rubūbiyyah and al-Ulūhiyyah at all.”
        </p>
        <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic leading-relaxed pl-2 border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pt-2">
          <strong>At-Ṭabarī:</strong> “(Explaining 47:19) Know that there is none worthy of worship who is befitting and deserving of al-Ulūhiyyah... except Allah, the One who created the creation. Everything besides Him must worship Him with ar-Rubūbiyyah.”
        </p>
      </div>

      <!-- At-Tahawee & Ibn Hibban -->
      <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#24211e] p-6 rounded-lg space-y-3">
        <div class="flex items-center justify-between border-b border-[#24211e] pb-2">
          <span class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Imām Aṭ-Ṭaḥāwī & Abū Ḥātim Ibn Ḥibbān</span>
          <span class="text-sm text-[#B3ADB9]">Early Affirmations</span>
        </div>
        <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic leading-relaxed pl-2 border-l-2 border-[#d6b278]">
          <strong>Aṭ-Ṭaḥāwī:</strong> “We say concerning the Tawḥīd of Allah: Allah is One without any associate, there is nothing similar to Him, and nothing can render Him incapable, and there is no deity besides Him.”
        </p>
        <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic leading-relaxed pl-2 border-l-2 border-[#d6b278] pt-2">
          <strong>Ibn Ḥibbān:</strong> “Praise is for Allah who is singled out with the oneness of al-Ulūhiyyah, exalted in might with the majesty of ar-Rubūbiyyah...”
        </p>
      </div>

      <!-- Ibn Abi Zayd & Ibn Battah -->
      <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#24211e] p-6 rounded-lg space-y-3">
        <div class="flex items-center justify-between border-b border-[#24211e] pb-2">
          <span class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Ibn Abī Zaydal-Qayrawānī & Ibn Baṭṭah</span>
          <span class="text-sm text-[#B3ADB9]">Threefold Classification</span>
        </div>
        <p class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic leading-relaxed pl-2 border-l-2 border-[#d6b278]">
          <strong>Ibn Baṭṭah al-‘Ukbarī:</strong> “The foundation of Īmān is in three things:
          <br>1. That the servant believes in His divinity (<em>Rabbāniyyah</em>) to be absolved from denial (<em>Ta‘ṭīl</em>).
          <br>2. That he believes in His oneness (<em>Waḥdāniyyah</em>) to be absolved from Shirk.
          <br>3. That he believes He is described with the Attributes that are obligatory for Him (Knowledge, Capability, Wisdom).”
        </p>
      </div>

      <!-- Refutation of Misconception -->
      <div class="bg-[#24211e] border border-[#24211e] p-6 rounded-lg space-y-3">
        <p class="text-sm font-mono uppercase tracking-widest text-[#d6b278]">Crucial Historical Note</p>
        <blockquote class="text-base sm:text-lg text-[#ECE8EF] italic leading-relaxed pl-2 border-none">
          All of these cited Imāms lived well before the time of Shaykh al-Islām Ibn Taymiyyah (رَحِمَهُ ٱللَّٰهُ), refuting the claim that these three categories were an innovation introduced by later scholars.
        </blockquote>
      </div>
